The principles

The rules every product is built to.

The products differ. The principles do not, because a bank, a regulator and an investor will test us on them.

01

Intelligence recommends, a named human decides

Nothing of consequence is filed or disbursed autonomously. That is what the regulator requires, and it is the product.

02

Own the data

Every decision, good or bad, is a labelled example kept for the next one. The labelled outcome data is the moat.

03

Create evidence, do not re-process records

The edge is in reading what others cannot read at the point of origination, not in re-processing records everyone already holds.

04

Build it inspectable

A buyer’s security team can read the product. Several platforms are single files by design, so the data does not leave the browser.

05

Claim only what is there

A deterministic engine is called a deterministic engine. A behavioural signal is called a behavioural signal. Defensible language is a competitive asset.

06

Asset light by design

We build the software and hold the intelligence and the data. The balance sheet risk sits with the lender.

01 · Front door

Origin8 Revenue OS

Autonomous revenue infrastructure that runs the whole cycle, from finding a prospect to the login and the funded product.

Live at origin8.litehouseiq.com

About 70 specialised agents8 autonomous stagesEmail, SMS, WhatsApp and voiceFinancial services and healthcare

What it is

An artificial intelligence agent orchestra for origination. Where a conventional aggregator ends at the handover of a lead, Origin8 carries the customer through qualification, engagement, login and know your customer, product match and the underwriting hand-off, and then learns from the outcome.

How it works

  1. Signals and prospect. Intent and first party data detect a prospect worth pursuing, and ideal customer cohorts are built and targeted.
  2. Engage and convert. Outreach runs across four channels, and the funnel is optimised continuously against outcomes.
  3. Login and match. The one time password and know your customer steps are orchestrated, and the lender or product is fitted to the applicant.
  4. Fund and learn. Underwriting is handed to Income360, and every outcome retrains the models, closing the loop.

Where it fits

The top of the funnel. It feeds the evidence engines and the operating system, and everything it learns about a cohort feeds the next targeting cycle.

02 · Evidence engine · Documents

DocLens

Document intelligence for Indian lending that runs inside the lender’s own network.

Live at doclens.litehouseiq.com

17 document types6 Indian language packs10 tampering checksNo per page chargeNo document leaves the network

What it is

DocLens reads the documents in a loan file, extracts the fields an underwriter uses, derives the credit analytics, and flags tampering. Recognition engines are commodities. The India specific layer above them, the statement layouts, the defensible derivations and the forgery forensics, is the product, and it deepens with every file.

How it works

  1. Ingest and classify. Each file is taken in and classified across seventeen document types.
  2. Extract and validate. Fields are extracted and checksums validated on capture, from the Aadhaar check digit to the GSTIN.
  3. Analyse. Bank statements are reconciled row by row, salary is detected by regularity, and obligations and average balances are derived.
  4. Check and route. Ten forgery checks run, and the file is routed straight through, to review, or to refer.

Where it fits

Every extracted value carries its page, its source text, its method and a confidence. That provenance is what lets a bank accept an automated read of a file.

03 · Evidence engine · Customer 360

Multimodal DeepTech

Assembles an interactive Customer 360 from every signal a borrower brings, and returns a trust and affordability read an underwriter can act on.

Live demonstration, access on request

Interactive Customer 360Multimodal signalsExplainable trust readA signal, not a verdict

What it is

A multimodal intelligence layer that assembles a single, interactive Customer 360 from documents, transactions, the public rails and the field record. It reads the whole customer rather than one data point, and returns a trust and ability signal created at the point of origination.

How it works

  1. Assemble. Every signal the customer brings is drawn into one interactive Customer 360 profile.
  2. Corroborate. Each attribute is cross checked against the consented data rails, so a figure resolves to a source rather than a claim.
  3. Score and explain. The signals fuse into one trust and affordability read with an explainable trail, for a named human to act on.

Where it fits

Paired with the ability read from Business X-Ray inside Income360, it completes the picture an underwriter needs.

04 · Evidence engine · Ability

Business X-Ray

Rebuilds turnover, margin and the working capital cycle for a business that keeps no accounts.

Live at bxray.litehouseco.com

56 trade profilesThree turnover scenariosEvidence strength on every lineNo individual record stored

What it is

The credit is not bad. The evidence of it is physical, and nobody has built the means to read it. Business X-Ray reads the shop, the stock, the machinery, the people at work and the behaviour of the owner, and converges them with GST and banking data where they exist into one assessed income, with a confidence band attached.

How it works

  1. Guided capture. The trade is chosen from fifty six profiles, and photographs are captured live from inside the field agent geofence, each read on the spot and confirmed by a person.
  2. Scenario reconstruction. A conservative, a base and an optimistic turnover are produced, and the interval narrows as evidence arrives.
  3. Cash flow rebuild. A monthly cash flow is rebuilt bottom up, and every line carries a score for how well it is corroborated.

Where it fits

The ability read inside Income360. It is triangulated with the Customer 360 read, the rails and the field before a named credit officer decides.

05 · Operating system

Income360

The origination to servicing operating system, from the field agent’s first contact on the phone to the last instalment.

Live at psl.litehouseco.com

38 consented data connectors18 stage lifecycleAbout 22 role dashboardsHuman gates at approval and disbursal

What it is

Where the evidence engines, the scorecards, the credit engine, the field roles and the compliance spine come together into one loan lifecycle. If the evidence engines answer the parts, Income360 is the whole.

How it works

  1. Credit engine. A bureau and affordability model turns the reads into one decision to approve or decline.
  2. Hard gates. Clear title and a minimum distress value are enforced regardless of score, alongside obligation and loan to value caps.
  3. Credit memo. An eight section appraisal memo fills as the case advances, and a named human signs it.

Where it fits

The assembly point of the whole stack. Every score resolves to a source, every decision to a named human, and every file to a labelled outcome that improves the next.

06 · Compliance

Ikigai

The compliance, credit and audit operating system for India’s regulated financial institutions.

Live at litehouseiq.com

15,125 regulatory documents107 return definitions32 executable rule setsNBFCs, HFCs, banks and insurers

What it is

A continuous compliance system: a deterministic policy engine, continuously recomputed, that keeps the institution current with its filings and its prudential floors across RBI, SEBI, NHB and DPDP, and across GST, income tax, PF, ESI, PT and company law. It is not an agentic system, and we do not describe it as one.

How it works

  1. Regulatory library. Every instrument from fifteen issuing authorities, each with a reference card.
  2. Submission universe and data room. Returns scoped to the entity type, fed from one canonical repository in the automated data flow shape.
  3. Prudential rule engine. Policy as code. A breach blocks the compliance officer’s sign-off until it is justified by name.
  4. Regulator format output. XBRL, tax network and statutory files generated for the officer to upload. The Praman layer runs the DPDP lifecycle over WhatsApp.

Where it fits

The compliance layer under the whole business. Nothing files autonomously: a named human signs every submission, a different human checks it, and the chain is hashed.

07 · Simulation

TwinSim

A synthetic population digital twin of Indian financial behaviour. Test it on the country before you test it on a customer.

Live at twinsim.litehouseiq.com

Household to nation8 operational agentsAppend-only decision ledgerNo customer data, DPDP safe

What it is

Statistically faithful synthetic citizens, built from public and licensed survey distributions, so a product, a price, a message or a policy can be tested against how the country actually behaves before it reaches a real person.

How it works

  1. The synthetic population. Resolved from a single household up to the nation.
  2. The orchestration. A conductor routes each task to specialised agents for compliance, credit, fraud, reporting, customer intelligence, insurance, microfinance and market simulation.
  3. The arbitration. Compliance and fraud hold veto authority, and ties or low confidence escalate to a human.
  4. The ledger. Every action, model version and human override is written to an append-only record.

Where it fits

It lets a portfolio built on the rest of the stack be stress tested before it is written, and its governance native design is the reference for how a regulated institution runs agents at all.
