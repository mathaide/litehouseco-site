The work

What a Mitra does, and what he never does.

A Mitra is paid across the life of the loan, not at disbursal. He gains nothing by pushing a customer into a loan that does not suit him.

01

A Mitra does

  • Introduces a business owner who needs a loan against property
  • Helps him through the Litehouse app
  • Stays in touch with him as his relationship manager for the life of the loan
02

A Mitra never

  • Takes cash, a fee or a gift from any customer
  • Promises a loan before the app approves it
  • Keeps customer papers or data outside the app

Who can join

People the neighbourhood already trusts.

  • Lives in the Mumbai-Thane or Pune region, for 5 years or more
  • Works in a financial role: pygmy (daily deposit) agent of a credit cooperative, tax or licence facilitation agent, bank Business Correspondent agent, or insurance or wealth advisor
  • 25 or older, 10th pass, with no criminal record
  • Has a smartphone, a PAN and a bank account in his own name
